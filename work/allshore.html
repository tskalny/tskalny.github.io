Web & Print · UI / UX

Allshore Talent

A visual identity across two deliverables for a global technology-staffing company — a corporate website system and a printed marketing booklet — translating a broad service offering (onshoring, nearshoring, offshoring and technology-as-a-service) into clear, trust-building design.

Client
Allshore Talent
Group
Cloud Primero
Role
UI/UX Designer · Freelance
Tools

Overview

The brief was to make global tech staffing feel controlled and credible across both screen and print. The website is a flexible page system anchored by a strong editorial hero, scannable service modules, proof through stats and client logos, and a recurring four-step rhythm.

The booklet carries the same brand language into a printed format — a “4 Pillars of Success” story told through bold type, generous imagery and consistent data points.
