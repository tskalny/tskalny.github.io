Brooks England

A redesign exploration for Brooks England, the heritage British maker of leather saddles and bags — bringing catalog, product and store-locator experiences into a calm, product-first layout for desktop and mobile.

Overview

The focus was letting craftsmanship lead. Generous product imagery, quiet typography and a restrained palette put the goods front and centre, while filtering, product detail and store-locator flows stay effortless to scan.

Mobile screens carry the same hierarchy down to a single column without losing the editorial feel.
